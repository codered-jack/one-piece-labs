This is the lab for the ***Volumes: keep your data*** lesson. This machine has Docker installed, and `~/wanted` holds the `wanted.py` app. You'll keep files safe outside your containers:

- keep a ship's **logbook** in a **volume**
- remove the container that wrote it, and see the logbook survive
- share a folder from this machine with a **bind mount**
- make a mount **read-only**
- clean up containers and volumes

Each step ends with a **Check** button that looks at your containers and volumes and tells you whether the task is done. Stuck? Every step has a **Tip**, and a **Solution** if you need it.

Press **Start** when you're ready.
