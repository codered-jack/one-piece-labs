This is the lab for the ***Share it: registries and tags*** lesson. This machine has Docker installed, and the folder `~/wanted` holds the wanted-poster app with its Dockerfile from the ***Build your own image*** lesson. You'll:

- start a **registry** of your own, at `localhost:5000`
- **push** version 1.0 of `wanted` to it
- ship version 1.1, and move the **`latest`** tag to it
- **pin** version 1.0 by its digest, so it can never change under you

You won't need an account anywhere: the registry runs on this machine.

Each step ends with a **Check** button that asks your registry whether the task is done. Stuck? Every step has a **Tip**, and a **Solution** if you need it.

Press **Start** when you're ready.
