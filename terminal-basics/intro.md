# Your terminal in 20 minutes

This is the lab for lesson 0. You'll practise the four ideas from the lesson on a real Linux machine:

- start a **process** in the background and find its PID
- see which **ports** are being listened on
- move a server from **localhost** to every address
- configure an app with an **environment variable**

Each step ends with a **Check** button that looks at the machine and tells you whether the task is done. Stuck? Every step has a **Tip**, and a **Solution** if you need it.

Press **Start** when you're ready.
